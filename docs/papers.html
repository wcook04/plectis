The papers

Eight short papers, one per problem, come first. The system papers explain how the work is checked and shared. The long reasoning records carry the complete route behind each short paper.

For a particular problem, choose it in the mathematics library to see its papers alongside its Lean proofs. Each entry below opens to the abstract, what the paper is and is not authority for, and the LaTeX behind the hosted PDF.

Erdős #68Short paper15 pages

Two Incomparable Denominator Exclusions for ∑n≥2(n!−1)−1

What exact denominator obstructions are checked for Erdős #68, and which quantified producer could still prove irrationality?

Read online · Open the PDF · LaTeX source · plectis-erdos

Abstract and reading guide

The paper proves that if S=a/q is rational, q cannot divide 299999! and is at least 2^39990 > 10^12038. The first exclusion comes from the exact strict-successor carry test and the second from continued fractions. Lean also checks the equivalent carry formulation and several consequences. A large common denominator alone does not control the reduced partial sums. Irrationality is reduced to a cofinal miss of the strict factorial successor, which remains open.

Erdős #243Short paper12 pages

Excluding the Bounded Negative Part

Which bounded negative-error behaviours can be excluded for Erdős #243, and which analytic hypotheses still keep it open?

Read online · Open the PDF · LaTeX source · plectis-erdos

Abstract and reading guide

Clearing denominators turns Erdős #243 into a centred integer error orbit. Normalised vanishing together with an eventually bounded negative part forces eventual Sylvester recurrence; for the canonical orbit, Koizumi supplies normalised vanishing. Absorption and descent are also identified with Koizumi's prior lemmas in canonical coordinates, with no priority claimed over them. Stabilisation of the common divisor and a Chinese remainder block give the final obstruction. The required negative-part bound, or the corresponding weighted record budget in the LCM formulation, remains unproved.

Erdős #249Short paper11 pages

A Basis for the 2-Kernel of Euler's Totient

What is the exact rank of the totient kernel at every integer base, which parts are formalised, and why does Erdős #249 remain open?

Read online · Open the PDF · LaTeX source · plectis-erdos

Abstract and reading guide

For every integer base k≥2, including composite bases, the totient kernel through level e≥1 has an explicit rational basis of size k^e + 1; level 0 has dimension 1. The paper gives an ordinary proof using arithmetic independence. Lean also proves all-base canonical-family independence and the complete rank unconditionally by CRT and Dirichlet. These coefficient-space results do not decide irrationality. A 240-bit Farey window excludes every reduced denominator through 79,639,646,646,701,375,323,355,774,875,831,053, and exact diagonal certificates exist for every t through 82. No t = 83 certificate or cofinal certificate supply is proved; Erdős #249 remains open.

problem-specific mathematical exposition for Erdős #249: the all-base exact finite-level rank and its formalisation boundary, infinite-dimensionality consequence, denominator exclusion, method limits, and open edge.
Not authority for
the validity of claims tagged Lean, which belongs to the cited kernel-checked source, or a solution to Erdős #249, which remains open.

Erdős #251Short paper20 pages

A Countermodel for Growth-and-Parity Arguments on the Prime-Gap Dyadic Series

What tail-shift condition would prove irrationality in Erdős #251, which natural affine schedules provably cannot supply it, and what prime-gap input still survives?

Read online · Open the PDF · LaTeX source · plectis-erdos

Abstract and reading guide

The paper identifies the complete prime-gap tails and classifies rationality by integrality of their shifts. Two adjacent shifts in (-1,1) with unequal associated gaps cannot both be integral, so their cofinal occurrence for every positive shift would prove irrationality. A positive even polynomial word has integral shifts everywhere, and a sparse rationalising perturbation of the actual prime gaps preserves every fixed eventual congruence and short-block empirical distribution while keeping small corrections. These countermodels delimit growth, parity, congruence, and local-statistics arguments; the prime-specific cofinal small-shift assertion remains open.

Erdős #257Short paper19 pages

Weighted Support Criteria for Reciprocal Mersenne Subseries

Which Mersenne-support subseries are settled, what finite-period and achievement-set structure is proved, and what still blocks Erdős #257?

Read online · Open the PDF · LaTeX source · plectis-erdos

Abstract and reading guide

Fix a finite nonempty prime set P and put h(a)=∏_{p∈P}p^{v_p(a)}. If an infinite support A has ∑_{a∈A}h(a)/(a(2^{h(a)}−1)) finite, its reciprocal Mersenne subseries is irrational at every integer base b≥2, as is the subseries on every infinite subset of A. The paper prints a complete ordinary proof and a host with divergent reciprocal sum, so the criterion is strictly weaker than reciprocal summability. A common observation window also proves hereditary irrationality for unions of weighted supports and strengthened positive-cover supports. These are ordinary results, not Lean theorems, and no priority claim is made. Universal Erdős #257 remains open.

problem-specific mathematical exposition for Erdős #257: exact finite-prime weighted support criterion, mixed weighted-and-cover support theorem and their ordinary proofs, exact finite periods, settled support families, method limits, and the open half-value edge.

the validity of claims tagged Lean, which belongs to the cited kernel-checked source, or a solution to Erdős #257, which remains open.

Erdős #269Short paper11 pages

No Finite Separable Representation at Three Prime Generators

For Erdős #269, which two-prime running-LCM sums are transcendental, what infinite-rank structure is proved at three primes, and which escape condition remains open?

Read online · Open the PDF · LaTeX source · plectis-erdos

Abstract and reading guide

For every pair of distinct primes, both the repeated running-LCM reciprocal sum and the version retaining one term per distinct LCM value are transcendental, by an ordinary deduction from a cited Hecke–Mahler theorem. Steve Fan posted the repeated-sum factorisation, reduction and transcendence conclusion first; the paper claims no priority or Lean formalisation for that case. For three distinct primes, the paper proves that the reciprocal running-LCM kernel has nonsingular minors of every order, with one index choice valid in every third-coordinate layer. After row and column powers are removed, a binary carry gives disjoint threshold intervals; this proves arbitrary-order non-separability and the exact rank of every finite sample. The residue-escape condition and repeated three-prime irrationality question remain open.

the problem-specific exposition for Erdős #269: the two-prime transcendence deductions and attribution, three-prime product formula, cell and shell structure, non-separability, finite experiments, and remaining cofinal escape condition.

the validity of claims tagged Lean, which belongs to the cited kernel-checked source, or a solution to Erdős #269, which remains open.

Erdős #1041Short paper22 pages

Sharp Solved Families and Constant-Factor Paths in Polynomial Lemniscates

Which explicit short-path families and critical-value estimates are proved for Erdős #1041, and which of those statements have Lean or Comparator checks?

Read online · Open the PDF · LaTeX source · plectis-erdos

Abstract and reading guide

The paper gives ordinary proofs of the length-less-than-2 conclusion for monic trinomials z^n+a z^m+b, with 1≤m<n and all roots in the open unit disc. It also proves that distinct concyclic zeros in degree n≥3 admit an adjacent chord shorter than 2 in {|f|<1} when their circle radius ρ satisfies 2ρ^n≤1; this alternation proof is not formalised in Lean. For squarefree monic polynomials, if the least critical-value modulus μ is at most 13/25, there is also such a connector. Without a root-location restriction, the scaled result gives a path shorter than (5/2) μ^(1/n) in {|f|<(25/13)μ}. Separate length-area and critical-value estimates give further control. Exact counterexamples show why these ingredients do not yield the unrestricted connector theorem; Erdős #1041 remains open.

the problem-specific exposition for Erdős #1041: ordinary all-degree trinomial paths, bounded-radius concyclic chords, a sharp critical-value mean, subsidiary Lean and Comparator checks, and exact obstructions that prevent promoting those estimates to a general connector theorem.

the validity of claims tagged Lean, which belongs to the cited kernel-checked source, or a solution to Erdős #1041, which remains open.

Erdős #1049Short paper14 pages

Irrationality of F(31/4) and the Exact Normalized Hankel Order

For Erdős #1049, which Lambert values at rational bases are proved irrational, what exact Hankel invariant does it compute, and why does the method still miss 3/2?

Read online · Open the PDF · LaTeX source · plectis-erdos

Abstract and reading guide

For coprime integers a>b≥1, the paper proves irrationality of F(a/b) when log b / log a is below the explicit threshold (approximately 0.40568); in particular F((31/4)^r) is irrational for every positive integer r, with a uniform irrationality-exponent bound. This is an ordinary proof using Zudilin's polynomial conclusion; Lean checks finite subclaims rather than the irrationality theorem. A second construction proves the exact normalised Hankel order and leading coefficient. The criterion does not cover 3/2, where a primitive noncollapsed kernel and analytic remainder control are still needed.

the problem-specific exposition for Erdős #1049: the ordinary rational-base irrationality theorem and uniform exponent bound for the (31/4)^r family, the exact normalised Hankel order and leading coefficient, the content and endpoint obstructions, and the remaining approximant and remainder construction at 3/2.

System paperAgent navigation11 pages

From a Cold Clone to a Proof Receipt

How can a reasoning agent comprehend a large Lean corpus before compiling, then cross into replayable proof authority and incremental validation?

Read online · Open the PDF · LaTeX source · plectis-erdos

Abstract and reading guide

A large formal library can be mechanically exact and still be practically unreadable to the next reader or reasoning agent. At the audited revision a cold clone exposes a bounded six-line tour over 1,019 Lean modules and 153,238 declarations, a map of ten programmes, 101 curated claims, and an elaborated reference graph, none of which requires a build. 503 of those modules and 8,171 of those declarations are marked machine-generated certificate shards, counted as source and never as claims, and that marking is a classification floor rather than the true generated share, since earlier emitted families predate the markers. Navigation receives no proof authority: probe verdicts come from the pinned Lean process and cannot be authored by the agent, and a claim must cite an accepted probe that replay reruns from stored bytes. The audit was author-operated; no external contributor had completed the tour or documented return path by 31 August 2026.

Explains
the agent-navigation architecture: layered corpus projections, bounded intent routing, kernel-authored session receipts, focused builds, and exact dependency-index cache receipts.
Not authority for
proof validity, optimal reasoning, external mathematical novelty, or demonstrated transfer to another formalisation project.
Where to start
problem, tour, authority, dogfood, limits (our selection, not the paper's own advice)

System paperOpen-source research strategy21 pages

From Spare Compute to Cumulative Mathematics

How can outsiders contribute compute, mathematical direction, architecture, or review without receiving authority to declare a proof?

Read online · Open the PDF · LaTeX source · plectis-erdos

Abstract and reading guide

A public Lean repository can serve as a shared conversion layer between compute, agent capability, human mathematical direction, infrastructure quality, and review. The paper defines role-bounded work packets, two first-class contribution tracks, provenance and credit, safe untrusted returns, and a candidate-to-attention funnel. Formal verification strengthens one evidence layer; human meaning, novelty, significance, and broad mathematical adoption remain separate. The eight Erdős problems are hard test worlds and all remain open. This is a working prototype; no completed external cold clone or landed outside contribution was recorded by 31 August 2026.

the open-source strategy: how outside compute, mathematics, architecture improvements, review, and credit enter one bounded commons.

System paperEvidence boundary22 pages

Plectis: What a Stranger Can Check

What may a stranger conclude from public evidence when the author chose both what to publish and what counts as a pass?

Read online · Open the PDF · LaTeX source · plectis

Abstract and reading guide

The author publishes selected code, fixed inputs, expected results, decision rules, and run records while the larger system stays private. What can a stranger conclude after rerunning them? A matching result shows repeatability for one stated public case. It does not establish where the code came from, whether the expected answer is correct, whether the published selection resembles the private whole, or whether the private system works reliably. The contribution is a method for making one selected disagreement local, not a score.

Explains
the evidence boundary: what a passing public run does and does not establish.
Not authority for
the private system's internal state, which is not public, and the mathematical results it cites as evidence.
Where to start
