Principles

  • Recompute, do not echo P-1
  • Lower claim strength to checker strength P-2
  • Concentrate trust in small checkers P-3
  • Possession is not permission P-4
  • Cache by content, not by name P-5
  • Status fails closed P-6
  • Track known unknowns without claiming the unknown is mapped P-7
  • Refuse inadmissible computations with typed reasons P-8
  • Preserve provenance across every boundary P-9
  • Do not land effects without compensation P-10
  • Bind volatile facts to refresh routes P-11
  • Make doctrine executable before authoritative P-12
  • Apply the same floor to meta artifacts P-13
  • Carry basis and provenance together P-14
  • Keep projections below source authority P-15
  • Bind authority to transaction scope P-16
  • Anchor graph mutations to unique source rows P-17
  • Require fan-in before activation P-18
  • Classify residual pressure before wiring P-19
  • Bind result records before record authority P-20

Axioms

  • Derivation before assertion AX-1
  • Kernelized verification AX-2
  • Authority by derivation, not possession AX-3
  • Content-addressed determinism AX-4
  • Fail-closed monotone lattice AX-5
  • Open-world epistemics AX-6
  • Typed partiality and refusal AX-7
  • Provenance propagation and non-interference AX-8
  • Compensable transactional effects AX-9
  • Temporal validity and freshness contracts AX-10
  • Executable grammar before doctrine authority AX-11
  • Reflexive accountability / no privileged meta-layer AX-12

  • Inadmissible number emission AP-6
  • Public/private membrane breach AP-7
  • Blind irreversible mutation AP-8
  • Frozen live fact AP-9
  • Prose-as-executable-authority AP-10
  • Meta-artifact exemption AP-11

  • Projection-as-source AP-17
