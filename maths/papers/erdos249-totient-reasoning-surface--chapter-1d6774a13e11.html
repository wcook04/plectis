Long paper

The Binary Totient Series

Erdős #249 By Will Cook July 2026; revised 1 October 2026 Authorship, AI use and citation
PDFOpen the PDF, 163 pages TeXLaTeX source Source on GitHub Problem page

Précis. The dyadic totient sections through level 𝑒 ≥1e≥1 have an explicit rational basis of size 2𝑒 +12e+1. Truncating a scaled tail difference produces an integer whose residue certifies nonintegrality when its distances from both endpoints of the residue interval exceed an explicit error bound. The record develops the resulting finite denominator exclusions, equivalent residue criteria, and sufficient exponential-sum estimates. Rational comparison sequences identify the limitations of particular size, parity, rank, and finite-prefix arguments. The short paper supplies the all-base basis theorem; here each conditional result carries the arithmetic hypothesis still needed to apply it. Those hypotheses are not established in the form required to prove irrationality of S=∑ 𝜑(𝑛)2−𝑛φ(n)2−n.

This paper owns a claim-bounded problem-specific reasoning surface for Erdős #249: selected checked premises, finite certificates, closed routes, surviving obligations, and their exact evidence bands.

It is not authority for the validity of claims tagged Lean, which belongs to the cited kernel-checked source, or a solution to Erdős #249, which remains open.

Approximating the nondivisor terms

The terms with 𝑑 ∣𝐻d∣H form a finite rational contribution to 𝑅2𝐻 −𝑅𝐻R2H−RH. To use it, we must also approximate the terms with 𝑑 ∤𝐻d∤H. The approximation and its error are explicit; for a cutoff 𝐷 ≥2𝐻D≥2H, only separation of the resulting rational centre from the integers remains to be checked.

For 𝑑 ≥1d≥1 and 𝑁 ≥0N≥0, let 𝑎𝑑(𝑁) =𝑑 −(𝑁mod𝑑)ad(N)=d−(Nmodd) be the least positive shift from 𝑁N to a multiple of 𝑑d. Summing the multiples of 𝑑d in the totient tail gives the rational term

𝜅𝑑(𝑁)=𝜇(𝑑)2𝑑−𝑎𝑑(𝑁)(𝑁+𝑎𝑑(𝑁)𝑑(2𝑑−1)+1(2𝑑−1)2),𝑅𝑁=∑𝑑≥1𝜅𝑑(𝑁).κd(N)=μ(d)2d−ad(N)(N+ad(N)d(2d−1)+1(2d−1)2),RN=∑d≥1κd(N).

Indeed, substitute 𝜑(𝑛) =∑𝑑∣𝑛𝜇(𝑑)𝑛/𝑑φ(n)=∑d∣nμ(d)n/d into 𝑅𝑁RN. For a fixed 𝑑d, the admissible shifts are 𝑎𝑑(𝑁) +𝑘𝑑ad(N)+kd, 𝑘 ≥0k≥0; their contribution is 𝜇(𝑑)∑𝑘≥0((𝑁 +𝑎𝑑(𝑁))/𝑑 +𝑘)2−𝑎𝑑(𝑁)−𝑘𝑑μ(d)∑k≥0((N+ad(N))/d+k)2−ad(N)−kd, which is the displayed expression. The interchange of sums is justified by

∑𝑗≥12−𝑗∑𝑑∣𝑁+𝑗|𝜇(𝑑)|𝑁+𝑗𝑑≤∑𝑗≥1(𝑁+𝑗)22−𝑗<∞.∑j≥12−j∑d∣N+j|μ(d)|N+jd≤∑j≥1(N+j)22−j<∞.

Thus subtraction and finite splitting of these series are valid. The terms are signed: for example, 𝜅2(0) = −4/9κ2(0)=−4/9. This identity is proved in Lean (Erdos249257.TotientShiftedMobiusForeignBridge.totientTail_eq_tsum_positiveForeignResidueKernel).

Proposition 6.142 (The finite divisor sum). For an integer 𝐻 >0H>0, the contribution of the divisor indices to 𝑅2𝐻 −𝑅𝐻R2H−RH is

𝐴𝐻=𝐻∑𝑑∣𝐻𝜇(𝑑)𝑑(2𝑑−1).AH=H∑d∣Hμ(d)d(2d−1).

For 𝑑 ∣𝐻d∣H, both 𝑎𝑑(𝐻)ad(H) and 𝑎𝑑(2𝐻)ad(2H) equal 𝑑d. Hence 𝜅𝑑(2𝐻) −𝜅𝑑(𝐻) =𝐻𝜇(𝑑)/(𝑑(2𝑑 −1))κd(2H)−κd(H)=Hμ(d)/(d(2d−1)). Summing over the divisors gives 𝐴𝐻AH, not the whole tail difference: the nondivisor terms must still be included.

For 𝐻 >0H>0 and an integer cutoff 𝐷 ≥0D≥0, set

𝑃𝐻,𝐷=∑1≤𝑑≤𝐷𝑑∤𝐻(𝜅𝑑(2𝐻)−𝜅𝑑(𝐻)),𝜀𝐻,𝐷=2𝐻(2𝐻−1)(22𝐷+43⋅4𝐷).PH,D=∑1≤d≤Dd∤H(κd(2H)−κd(H)),εH,D=2H(2H−1)(22D+43⋅4D).

Here 𝐴𝐻AH includes all divisor indices, even when 𝐷 <𝐻D<H. The following implication therefore keeps the error estimate as an explicit hypothesis; it will be proved below when 𝐷 ≥2𝐻D≥2H.

Proposition 6.143 (Separation larger than the error implies exclusion). Suppose

| | |(𝑅2𝐻−𝑅𝐻)−(𝐴𝐻+𝑃𝐻,𝐷)| | |≤𝜀𝐻,𝐷,|𝐴𝐻+𝑃𝐻,𝐷−𝑧|>𝜀𝐻,𝐷for every 𝑧∈ℤ.|(R2H−RH)−(AH+PH,D)|≤εH,D,|AH+PH,D−z|>εH,Dfor every z∈Z.

Then 𝑅2𝐻 −𝑅𝐻 ∉ℤR2H−RH∉Z.

Proof. If 𝑅2𝐻 −𝑅𝐻 =𝑧 ∈ℤR2H−RH=z∈Z, the two hypotheses give opposite inequalities for |𝐴𝐻 +𝑃𝐻,𝐷 −𝑧||AH+PH,D−z|. ◻

Why the error hypothesis holds for 𝐷 ≥2𝐻D≥2H.

Every divisor of 𝐻H then lies below the cutoff. For 𝑑 >𝐷d>D, both 𝐻H and 2𝐻2H are smaller than 𝑑d, so the omitted increment simplifies to

𝜅𝑑(2𝐻)−𝜅𝑑(𝐻)=𝜇(𝑑)2𝐻(2𝐻−1)(12𝑑−1+1(2𝑑−1)2).κd(2H)−κd(H)=μ(d)2H(2H−1)(12d−1+1(2d−1)2).

Since |𝜇(𝑑)| ≤1|μ(d)|≤1 and 2𝑑 −1 ≥2𝑑−12d−1≥2d−1,

| | |(𝑅2𝐻−𝑅𝐻)−(𝐴𝐻+𝑃𝐻,𝐷)| | |≤2𝐻(2𝐻−1)∑𝑑>𝐷(22𝑑+44𝑑)=𝜀𝐻,𝐷.|(R2H−RH)−(AH+PH,D)|≤2H(2H−1)∑d>D(22d+44d)=εH,D.

Thus for 𝐻 >0H>0 and 𝐷 ≥2𝐻D≥2H the error bound needs no additional convergence hypothesis. The same estimate is proved in Lean (Erdos249257.TotientShiftedMobiusForeignBridge.controlledForeignProjection_of_twice_le); together with the displayed separation inequality, it gives the cited nonintegrality consequence (Erdos249257.TotientShiftedMobiusForeignBridge.scaleFullTarget_miss_of_projected_separation_of_twice_le_of_forall_int).

For example, at 𝐻 =1H=1 and 𝐷 =5D=5 the rational centre and radius are

𝐴1+𝑃1,5=304282423801,𝜀1,5=49384,12<𝐴1+𝑃1,5−𝜀1,5<𝐴1+𝑃1,5+𝜀1,5<1.A1+P1,5=304282423801,ε1,5=49384,12<A1+P1,5−ε1,5<A1+P1,5+ε1,5<1.

Thus this finite calculation excludes an integral value of 𝑅2 −𝑅1R2−R1. The cutoff restriction alone does not ensure success: at 𝐻 =1,𝐷 =4H=1,D=4, the centre 346/441346/441 is within the radius 25/9625/96 of 11. For fixed 𝐻H the radius tends to zero as 𝐷 →∞D→∞, but an irrationality argument still needs the required family of separated centres. Neither a single example nor convergence of the error supplies it.

When 𝐷 ≥2𝐻D≥2H, the first-order part of the omitted sum can also be included exactly: ∑𝑑>𝐷𝜇(𝑑)/(2𝑑 −1) =1/2 −∑𝑑≤𝐷𝜇(𝑑)/(2𝑑 −1)∑d>Dμ(d)/(2d−1)=1/2−∑d≤Dμ(d)/(2d−1). Adding 2𝐻(2𝐻 −1)2H(2H−1) times this rational correction to 𝐴𝐻 +𝑃𝐻,𝐷AH+PH,D gives the squared-Mersenne approximation above (Erdos249257.TotientShiftedMobiusForeignBridge.renormalizedResidueAgreement_of_twice_le). This explains the relation between the two approximations and the improved squared-denominator remainder; it does not provide their integer separation.
