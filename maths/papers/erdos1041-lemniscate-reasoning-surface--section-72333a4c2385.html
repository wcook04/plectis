Paths in Polynomial Lemniscates: A Degree-Seven Counterexample and Radial Connections

Erdős #1041 By Will Cook 29 September 2026
PDFOpen the PDF, 86 pages TeXLaTeX source Source on GitHub Problem dossier

Précis. For a monic trinomial with roots in the open unit disc, any two distinct roots are joined inside {|f|<1} by a path of length less than 2. For any squarefree monic polynomial of degree 𝑛 ≥2n≥2, an area-growth argument gives such a path when the least critical-value modulus μ is at most 13/2513/25; scaling gives length less than (5/2)𝜇1/𝑛(5/2)μ1/n inside {|𝑓| <(25/13)𝜇}{|f|<(25/13)μ}. The record develops this argument, an inverse-ray averaging estimate, and a construction using an isolated simple critical value, together with results for collinear roots and several coefficient families. A weighted Poisson identity gives a sharp mean bound on critical values; examples distinguish that bound from path-length control. The final sections examine inverse-sheet topology and refute an earlier spanning-tree estimate. These results do not prove the unrestricted length-2 assertion.

Trinomials

At a zero of a trinomial, the root equation eliminates the middle coefficient. Along the segment from the origin to that zero, the remaining expression is a nonnegative linear combination of two values of modulus less than one. The sum of the coefficients is at most one, which gives the required containment.

Theorem 3.1 (trinomial root connections). Let 𝑛,𝑚n,m be integers with 1 ≤𝑚 <𝑛1≤m<n, and let 𝑓(𝑧) =𝑧𝑛 +𝑎𝑧𝑚 +𝑏f(z)=zn+azm+b have every zero in 𝔻D. For every zero 𝜁ζ, the segment [0,𝜁][0,ζ] lies in 𝐸𝑓Ef. Consequently any two zeros 𝜁1,𝜁2ζ1,ζ2 are joined in 𝐸𝑓Ef by the broken line 𝜁1 →0 →𝜁2ζ1→0→ζ2, of length |𝜁1| +|𝜁2| <2|ζ1|+|ζ2|<2.

Proof. Vieta’s formula gives |𝑏| <1|b|<1. At a zero 𝜁ζ the root equation 𝜁𝑛 +𝑎𝜁𝑚 +𝑏 =0ζn+aζm+b=0 eliminates the middle coefficient:

𝑓(𝑡𝜁)=𝑏(1−𝑡𝑚)+𝜁𝑛(𝑡𝑛−𝑡𝑚).(5)(5)f(tζ)=b(1−tm)+ζn(tn−tm).

For 0 ≤𝑡 <10≤t<1 the two scalar weights 1 −𝑡𝑚1−tm and 𝑡𝑚 −𝑡𝑛tm−tn are nonnegative and sum to 1 −𝑡𝑛1−tn, so

|𝑓(𝑡𝜁)|≤|𝑏|(1−𝑡𝑚)+|𝜁|𝑛(𝑡𝑚−𝑡𝑛)<1−𝑡𝑛≤1.|f(tζ)|≤|b|(1−tm)+|ζ|n(tm−tn)<1−tn≤1.

At 𝑡 =1t=1 the value is zero. Concatenating two such segments gives the length assertion. ◻

No separate bound on 𝑎a is needed, since the root equation eliminates it before we take absolute values. The conclusion concerns segments to zeros and makes no assertion that 𝐸𝑓Ef is star-shaped.

Summation by parts.

For 𝑓(𝑧) =∑𝑛𝑘=0𝑐𝑘𝑧𝑘f(z)=∑k=0nckzk and a zero 𝜁ζ, put 𝑆𝑗 =∑𝑗𝑘=0𝑐𝑘𝜁𝑘Sj=∑k=0jckζk. Finite summation by parts gives

The weights in (6) are nonnegative and sum to 1 −𝑡𝑛1−tn, so the whole radial segment lies in the closed unit sublevel set whenever every partial sum lies in the closed unit disc. For a trinomial, 𝑆𝑗 =𝑏Sj=b for 𝑗 <𝑚j<m and 𝑆𝑗 = −𝜁𝑛Sj=−ζn for 𝑚 ≤𝑗 <𝑛m≤j<n; these two values may coincide. This is exactly the estimate above. With two intermediate coefficients, the assumption that all zeros lie in the unit disc need not bound every partial sum by one. The next example shows that a prescribed radial segment can then escape.

𝑓𝑟(𝑟/2)=−327320𝑟6,fr(r/2)=−327320r6,

Sources and scope.
