Geometric Moments and Rational Lambert Values: Proofs and Further Results

Erdős #1049 By Will Cook September 2026 Authorship, AI use and citation
PDFOpen the PDF, 81 pages TeXLaTeX source Source on GitHub Problem page

Précis. Specialising Zudilin's 2004 linear forms shows F(a/b) irrational for coprime 𝑎 >𝑏 ≥1a>b≥1 when θ=log b / log a is less than θ^*=0.4056830213840605…, with irrationality-exponent bound (1−θ)/(θ*−θ) for every positive integral power. In particular the bound is less than 301 for every positive integral power of 31/431/4. The constants are Zudilin's; the proof cancels common cyclotomic factors before rational specialisation. For the 2016 Hankel construction with x=z=1, the first nonzero term of the normalised determinant is computed at every rank. A separate positive-measure argument proves positivity and a size estimate for each fixed real 0<q<1. The final sections explain the limits of the stated clearing and congruence arguments at 3/23/2: no small nonzero sequence of divided remainders is constructed there, and the full rational-base conjecture remains open.

This paper owns the complete problem-specific reasoning surface for Erdős #1049, including all registered result families and their boundaries.

It is not authority for the validity of claims tagged Lean, which belongs to the cited kernel-checked source, or a solution to Erdős #1049, which remains open.

The range of subset sums.

The residue condition and the real-error condition can be counted together. Within each residue class, subdivide the range of subset remainders into intervals of width 𝐷/𝑛D/n; a bound on repeated remainder values then forces a nonzero small difference. Fix a positive integer 𝑛n. For 𝑀M integer rows (𝐴𝑗,𝐵𝑗)(Aj,Bj) and a modulus 𝐷 ≥1D≥1, set 𝑒𝑗 =𝐴𝑗𝐹(3/2) −𝐵𝑗ej=AjF(3/2)−Bj. For each attained residue vector 𝑏b, let 𝑇𝑏Tb be the difference between the largest and smallest remainders ∑𝑗𝜀𝑗𝑒𝑗∑jεjej with 𝜀𝑗 ∈{0,1}εj∈{0,1} and ∑𝑗𝜀𝑗(𝐴𝑗,𝐵𝑗) ≡𝑏(mod𝐷)∑jεj(Aj,Bj)≡b(modD). Let the integer 𝑘𝑏kb bound the number of subsets attaining any one remainder value in that residue class. Then

produces two subsets with equal residues and distinct remainders less than 𝐷/𝑛D/n apart. Within each residue fibre, subtract the least remainder, multiply by 𝑛/𝐷n/D, and take floors. These integer indices range from 00 to ⌊𝑛𝑇𝑏/𝐷⌋⌊nTb/D⌋, including a separate final index when 𝑇𝑏Tb is a positive integral multiple of 𝐷/𝑛D/n. Equal indices give a remainder difference strictly less than 𝐷/𝑛D/n. If each index corresponded to only one remainder value, it would account for at most 𝑘𝑏kb subsets, contradicting the displayed inequality. Subtracting the two subset sums and dividing their coefficients by 𝐷D gives an integer form with nonzero absolute value less than 1/𝑛1/n. The common width bound 𝑇 =∑𝑗|𝑒𝑗|T=∑j|ej| and a common multiplicity bound 𝑘k give the coarser count 𝑄𝑘(⌊𝑛𝑇/𝐷⌋ +1)Qk(⌊nT/D⌋+1), where 𝑄Q is the number of attained residue vectors. All rows must have the same normalisation. Primitive rows can still have repeated subset sums, and positivity need not survive subtraction.
