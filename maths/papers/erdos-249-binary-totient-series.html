A Basis for the 2-Kernel of Euler's Totient

Erdős #249 11 pp Equations typeset from the exact TeX
PDFOpen the PDF · 11 pp TeXLaTeX source Source on GitHub Problem dossier

Précis

For every integer base k≥2, including composite bases, the totient kernel through level e≥1 has an explicit rational basis of size ke + 1; level 0 has dimension 1. The paper gives an ordinary proof using arithmetic independence. Lean proves the dyadic rank unconditionally; its all-base rank theorem assumes the canonical family's independence. These coefficient-space results do not decide irrationality. A 240-bit Farey window excludes every reduced denominator through 79,639,646,646,701,375,323,355,774,875,831,053, and exact diagonal certificates exist for every t through 82. No t = 83 certificate or cofinal certificate supply is proved; Erdős #249 remains open.

This paper owns problem-specific mathematical exposition for Erdős #249: the all-base exact finite-level rank and its formalisation boundary, infinite-dimensionality consequence, denominator exclusion, method limits, and open edge.

It is not authority for the validity of claims tagged Lean, which belongs to the cited kernel-checked source, or a solution to Erdős #249, which remains open.

The evaluation matrix

The two ingredients are scalar reduction and arithmetic independence. The reductions explain which sections must be dependent. The difficulty is to exclude every additional relation. We do this one coefficient at a time: an evaluation will annihilate all the other columns modulo a prime while leaving the chosen column nonzero. Thus no simultaneous primality assertion for several affine forms is needed. The local formula for 𝜑φ gives the displayed reductions. For a prime 𝑝 ∣𝑘p∣k that does not divide 𝑢u, the argument 𝑘𝑗−𝑡𝑛 +𝑢kj−tn+u is a 𝑝p-adic unit, so multiplication by 𝑘𝑡kt contributes its power of 𝑝p and one factor 1 −1/𝑝1−1/p. When 𝑝 ∣𝑢p∣u, the argument already contains 𝑝p, and only its power contributes. This gives 𝐶𝑘(𝑡,𝑢)Ck(t,u).

A single canonical endpoint

For 𝑐 ≥0c≥0, odd 𝑣 ≥1v≥1 and a positive multiple 𝐻H of 𝜑(𝑣)φ(v), put

Information that does not force the gap

  1. [exact reformulation] Actual LCM orbit frontier. Let 𝐻𝑎 =lcm⁡(1,…,2𝑎)Ha=lcm⁡(1,…,2a) and 𝑅𝑎 =totientTail⁡(2𝐻𝑎) −totientTail⁡(𝐻𝑎)Ra=totientTail⁡(2Ha)−totientTail⁡(Ha).   2. [unconditional progress] Positive LCM corridor and top-edge boundary.   3. [unconditional progress] LCM factor-ideal shift-algebra no-go. The witness is synthetic: its letters are not claimed to be actual totient differences, nonlinear combinations are outside the theorem, and no cofinal family of certificates follows. Antecedents and proof record

Allouche and Shallit introduced 𝑘k-regular sequences . Coons proved that 𝜑φ is not 𝑘k-regular [2]. Martin’s Theorem 1 makes the ratios of totients on any prescribed finite list of pairwise nonproportional affine forms with integer coefficients and positive slopes arbitrarily separated; this implies their linear independence [3]. Section 1.1 gives a direct determinant proof tailored to the sections used here. The basis theorem gives the exact finite-level normal form. Wong [4] proved least-residue irrationality when the denominator base and modulus are the same integer 𝑘 >2k>2; Section 2 fixes base two and classifies dyadic observables. The original question appears in Erdős and Graham [5].

The dyadic finite-level rank 2𝑒 +12e+1, residue-series irrationality, full-depth amplification, canonical equivalence and the positive rank-one floor are in the public snapshot. The unconditional all-base exact-rank theorem is not a Lean theorem without Martin’s independence hypothesis; the formal transfer is rank from an independent all-base family. The canonical gap is the remaining arithmetic endpoint. The historical source links retain 99f4bf47422a. The integral normal form has its ordinary proof above. The periodic transport lemma is checked in PeriodicTotientIndependence.lean; the eventual version follows by the displayed threshold shift. The rational control and the termwise-window and prime-dilation boundaries are proved in ParityPerturbedRationalControl.lean and PrefixValuationAndControlRigidity.lean, respectively. Finite evaluation ranks and the all-level carry lower bound remain distinct. The long record retains the separate continued-fraction bounds 𝑞 ≥239989q≥239989 and 𝑞 >1012038q>1012038 under rationality, alongside the Lean-checked Farey exclusion. Neither implies the universal gap.
