Paths in Polynomial Lemniscates: A Degree-Seven Counterexample and Radial Connections

Erdős #1041 By Will Cook 29 September 2026 Authorship, AI use and citation
PDFOpen the PDF, 86 pages TeXLaTeX source Source on GitHub Problem page

Précis. For a monic trinomial with roots in the open unit disc, any two distinct roots are joined inside {|f|<1} by a path of length less than 2. For any squarefree monic polynomial of degree 𝑛 ≥2n≥2, an area-growth argument gives such a path when the least critical-value modulus μ is at most 13/2513/25; scaling gives length less than (5/2)𝜇1/𝑛(5/2)μ1/n inside {|𝑓| <(25/13)𝜇}{|f|<(25/13)μ}. The record develops this argument, an inverse-ray averaging estimate, and a construction using an isolated simple critical value, together with results for collinear roots and several coefficient families. A weighted Poisson identity gives a sharp mean bound on critical values; examples distinguish that bound from path-length control. The final sections examine inverse-sheet topology and refute an earlier spanning-tree estimate. These results do not prove the unrestricted length-2 assertion.

A two-sheeted component

The quantity 𝛿 =1 −|𝑝(𝑐)|δ=1−|p(c)| measures the remaining length of the slit in the value plane. The local quadratic coefficient converts it into the spatial scale √𝛿/𝑀δ/M. The radius ℎh records where that quadratic comparison is valid. Degree two will turn the local preimage estimate into a statement about the whole component.

Lemma 2.1 (a bottleneck estimate). Let 𝑝p be a polynomial and 𝑈U a component of {|𝑝| <1}{|p|<1} on which 𝑝p is a proper map of degree two. Suppose its only critical point 𝑐c is simple and 𝑣 =𝑝(𝑐) ≠0v=p(c)≠0. Write

𝑝(𝑐+𝑧)−𝑣=𝑧2𝐴(𝑧),𝑀=|𝐴(0)|,𝛿=1−|𝑣|.p(c+z)−v=z2A(z),M=|A(0)|,δ=1−|v|.

For each 𝑤 ∈𝐽w∈J, Rouché’s theorem therefore gives two zeros, counted with multiplicity, of 𝑝(𝑐 +𝑧) −𝑤p(c+z)−w in |𝑧| <𝑟0|z|<r0. They remain in 𝑈U: as 𝑤w moves out from 𝑣v along 𝐽J, the zeros continue from 𝑐c and cannot cross |𝑧| =𝑟0|z|=r0. Since the degree on 𝑈U is two, these are all its preimages of 𝑤w. For 𝑟0 <𝑡 <|𝑎 −𝑐|r0<t<|a−c|, the set 𝑈1 ∩{|𝑧 −𝑐| >𝑡}U1∩{|z−c|>t} has relative boundary in 𝑈U contained in the circle |𝑧 −𝑐| =𝑡|z−c|=t. If that circle missed 𝐾 ∩𝑈1K∩U1, then 𝐾 ∩𝑈1 ∩{|𝑧 −𝑐| >𝑡}K∩U1∩{|z−c|>t} would be both open and closed in 𝐾K. It contains 𝑎a and excludes 𝑏b, contradicting connectedness.
