Which prime powers survive reduction

Erdős states the question on p. 102 of his 1988 survey and, in the same passage, records the expectation that ∑n1/(n!+t)\sum_n1/(n!+t) is irrational, indeed transcendental, for every integer tt [1]. Why one proposed window argument is circular

One route to cofinal non-unit carries tries to exclude two consecutive unit carries by extracting a prime-power or quotient-gcd obstruction from their cleared width-one window.
