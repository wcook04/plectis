Long paper

The Binary Totient Series

Erdős #249 By Will Cook July 2026; revised 1 October 2026 Authorship, AI use and citation
PDFOpen the PDF, 163 pages TeXLaTeX source Source on GitHub Problem page

Précis. The dyadic totient sections through level 𝑒 ≥1e≥1 have an explicit rational basis of size 2𝑒 +12e+1. Truncating a scaled tail difference produces an integer whose residue certifies nonintegrality when its distances from both endpoints of the residue interval exceed an explicit error bound. The record develops the resulting finite denominator exclusions, equivalent residue criteria, and sufficient exponential-sum estimates. Rational comparison sequences identify the limitations of particular size, parity, rank, and finite-prefix arguments. The short paper supplies the all-base basis theorem; here each conditional result carries the arithmetic hypothesis still needed to apply it. Those hypotheses are not established in the form required to prove irrationality of S=∑ 𝜑(𝑛)2−𝑛φ(n)2−n.

This paper owns a claim-bounded problem-specific reasoning surface for Erdős #249: selected checked premises, finite certificates, closed routes, surviving obligations, and their exact evidence bands.

It is not authority for the validity of claims tagged Lean, which belongs to the cited kernel-checked source, or a solution to Erdős #249, which remains open.

The actual tail difference on the LCM diagonal

For 𝐻 =𝐻2𝑎H=H2a, the actual difference Ω𝑎 =𝑅2𝐻 −𝑅𝐻Ωa=R2H−RH removes the auxiliary rational approximation from the LCM criterion. The equivalence below restricts attention to this sparse sequence of tails. Its required nonintegrality is still an arithmetic assertion to prove.

The exact equivalence.

𝑆∉ℚ⟺∀𝑎0, ∃𝑎≥𝑎0, Ω𝑎∉ℤ.S∉Q⟺∀a0, ∃a≥a0, Ωa∉Z.

The right-hand side is an equivalent reformulation of irrationality, not a weaker hypothesis already established by the finite examples. The following sufficient conditions are attempts to prove it.

Five sufficient conditions for that residue inequality

The next five quantified conditions each imply irrationality. Conditions (i)–(iv) reach the upper-endpoint test; (v) gives nonintegrality directly. Their proven relations are summarised in Proposition 6.65.

    𝑚+1+𝑎+6<2⋅2𝑎,2𝐻+𝑚+3<2𝑚,2𝐻+𝑚+2≤(𝐷(𝐻,𝐻,𝑚)+𝛿2𝑎(𝑚+1))mod2𝑚≤2𝑚−(2𝐻+𝑚+3),m+1+a+6<2⋅2a,2H+m+3<2m,2H+m+2≤(D(H,H,m)+δ2a(m+1))mod2m≤2m−(2H+m+3),

A further sufficient terminal-carry condition

With 𝐻 =𝐻2𝑎H=H2a, the unproved quantified condition is

∀𝑎0 ∃𝑎≥max(𝑎0,8) ∃𝑞:2𝑞+2+(𝑎+6)<2⋅2𝑎,2(𝐻+𝑞+2)≤4𝑞,2𝑢𝑎,𝑞≤𝛿2𝑎(2𝑞+2)−(2𝐻+2𝑞+3)or𝛿2𝑎(2𝑞+2)≤2𝑢𝑎,𝑞.∀a0 ∃a≥max(a0,8) ∃q:2q+2+(a+6)<2⋅2a,2(H+q+2)≤4q,2ua,q≤δ2a(2q+2)−(2H+2q+3)orδ2a(2q+2)≤2ua,q.

The proof assumes an integer 𝑧 =Ω𝑎z=Ωa and uses

2𝑢𝑎,𝑞=𝛿2𝑎(2𝑞+2)−𝑐𝐻,𝐻,𝑧(2𝑞+1).2ua,q=δ2a(2q+2)−cH,H,z(2q+1).

Erdos249257.DiagonalFreshLossBridge.PowerTwoOddWindowAffine.two_mul_actualOddHalfCenteredLift_eq_terminal_sub_trueCarry The carry is then a positive tail difference, strictly smaller than 2𝐻 +2𝑞 +32H+2q+3. The negative endpoint representative is minus this carry (Erdos249257.DiagonalFreshLossBridge.PowerTwoOddWindowAffine.actualLcm_trueEndpointSurvivor_neg). The first alternative contradicts the upper bound; the second contradicts positivity, which is the terminal-dominance argument (Erdos249257.DiagonalFreshLossBridge.PowerTwoOddWindowAffine.actualLcmTailOrbit_notMem_int_of_actualTerminalDominance).

Both arguments are conditional contradictions, not evidence against either unproved estimate. No converse from nonintegrality to either finite inequality is asserted. In particular, the exact identity must not be described as making this sufficient condition necessary.

Certificates in short windows

For every 𝑡,𝑗 ∈ℕt,j∈N, the arithmetic difference on the LCM progression is exactly

𝛿𝑡(𝑗)=𝜑(2𝐻𝑡+𝑗)−𝜑(𝐻𝑡+𝑗).δt(j)=φ(2Ht+j)−φ(Ht+j).

No coprimality restriction on 𝑗j is needed (Erdos249257.DiagonalFreshLossBridge.PowerTwoOddWindowAffine.lcmRayArithmeticLetter_eq_deltaTotient). Consequently the corresponding window numerator is

𝐷(𝐻𝑡,𝐻𝑡,𝐿)=𝐿∑𝑗=1𝛿𝑡(𝑗)2𝐿−𝑗,D(Ht,Ht,L)=∑j=1Lδt(j)2L−j,

and applying the same residue and error bounds gives precisely the test C(𝐻𝑡,𝐻𝑡,𝐿)C(Ht,Ht,L) (Erdos249257.DiagonalFreshLossBridge.PowerTwoOddWindowAffine.lcmDiagonalArithmeticKill_iff_certifiedKill). Thus the quantified assertion

∀𝑎0 ∃𝑎≥𝑎0 ∃𝐿<2⋅2𝑎,C(𝐻2𝑎,𝐻2𝑎,𝐿)∀a0 ∃a≥a0 ∃L<2⋅2a,C(H2a,H2a,L)

implies irrationality (Erdos249257.DiagonalFreshLossBridge.PowerTwoOddWindowAffine.PowerTwoActualLcmShortArithmeticKillSupply, Erdos249257.DiagonalFreshLossBridge.PowerTwoOddWindowAffine.actualLcmOrbitNonintegralitySupply_of_shortArithmeticKill); the assertion itself is unproved. It restricts the diagonal indices to powers of two and imposes the additional bound 𝐿 <2 ⋅2𝑎L<2⋅2a. Pointwise completeness allows an unspecified finite depth; it does not imply a witness within this short window.

The displayed certified examples have 𝑎 =4a=4, 𝐿 =23L=23 and 𝑎 =6a=6, 𝐿 =93L=93 (Erdos249257.DiagonalFreshLossBridge.PowerTwoOddWindowAffine.actualLcmTailOrbit_four_and_six_notMem_int); these are finite computations. It makes no assertion for larger thresholds, or that these are the only successful exponents.

A sufficient approximation bound

Put 𝐻 =𝐻2𝑎H=H2a and define the finite rational and its error bound by

𝜌𝑎,𝑞=𝐷(𝐻,𝐻,2𝑞+1)+𝛿2𝑎(2𝑞+2)22𝑞+1,𝜀𝑎,𝑞=2𝐻+2𝑞+322𝑞+1.ρa,q=D(H,H,2q+1)+δ2a(2q+2)22q+1,εa,q=2H+2q+322q+1.

|Ω𝑎−𝜌𝑎,𝑞|<𝜀𝑎,𝑞.|Ωa−ρa,q|<εa,q.
